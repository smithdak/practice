# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Practice is a working community for people making AI useful in real contexts. This Code of Conduct protects the conditions for that work: specific claims, honest uncertainty, respectful disagreement, and accountability for harm.

It applies to participation in Practice spaces, including Buzz, Git repositories, events, direct messages used for community work, and any representation made on behalf of the community.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat people as collaborators, even when challenging an idea, method, or result.
- Make critique specific to the work: name the claim, evidence, decision, or impact at issue.
- Distinguish observed evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, and personal experience.
- Respect consent, privacy, licenses, and confidentiality. Remove or redact sensitive material from a contribution before sharing it.
- Welcome different levels of experience and explain specialized terms when they matter to the discussion.
- Accept reasonable correction, repair harm when asked, and follow a moderator's direction while a report is being assessed.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are conduct violations:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Hate speech or demeaning treatment based on a person's identity, protected characteristic, disability, background, or perceived status.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, dogpiling, or repeated bad-faith engagement intended to drive someone from participation.
- Sharing personal, confidential, or identifying information without permission; requesting secrets, credentials, or private keys; or pressuring someone to disclose them.
- Plagiarism, knowingly false attribution, impersonation, or presenting fabricated evidence or results as real.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern, participates in a review, or supports a report in good faith.
- Deliberately disrupting community work, evading a human moderation decision, or using agents to amplify any prohibited behavior.

Good-faith disagreement, a correction, a declined contribution, or a request for evidence is not by itself a conduct violation.

## Reporting a concern

Use the private reporting route published in Practice's current community entry points. Do not put sensitive evidence in a public thread, issue, or pull request. If no private route is currently available, contact a named human maintainer through their published official contact method and state that the matter is a conduct report.

A useful report includes only what the reporter is comfortable sharing:

- what happened and where;
- dates or times, links, screenshots, or other available evidence;
- people involved or affected;
- any immediate safety or privacy concern; and
- the outcome the reporter is seeking, if known.

Reports may be made by an affected person or a witness. A report made in good faith will not be punished because it is unsubstantiated. Deliberately false reports may be handled as a conduct concern.

## What happens next

Human moderators handle reports under the [moderation model](community/MODERATION.md). They acknowledge receipt when a private reply path is available, assess immediate risk, preserve relevant evidence, seek only necessary context, and make or escalate a decision. The community does not promise a fixed resolution time because urgency, safety, and available evidence vary.

Participants may ask for a review of a decision through the appeal process in the moderation model. Retaliation during or after that process is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Human moderators may give guidance, issue a formal warning, require a pause from a discussion or space, limit participation for a defined period, or remove access. The response should be proportionate to the behavior, context, pattern, impact, and safety risk. No agent may ban a person, remove their access, or silently remove their content.

Where privacy and safety permit, a moderator explains the decision and its duration to the affected participant. Decisions and retained records are handled according to the moderation model.

## Scope and review

This policy is enforced by human maintainers and any human moderators they designate. It is a community operating rule, not a substitute for emergency services or legal advice. A human maintainer may revise it through a documented Git change; material changes should be announced in the active community spaces.
