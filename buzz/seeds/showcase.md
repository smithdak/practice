# Share what happened

Tell the smallest complete implementation story:

- **Before and constraint**
- **Change or implementation**
- **How it was checked**
- **Observed result or failure**
- **Evidence quality and limitation**
- **Reusable artifact and next step**

Negative results are welcome when they change the next decision. Distinguish
measurements, observations, and hypotheses. Remove sensitive details and link
the durable method, code, checklist, Lab, or Story when it is safe to share.
