# Maintainer queue

Open a maintainer item with:

`DECISION|REVIEW|RELEASE|MODERATION · context · evidence/link · recommendation · human owner/action`

Keep the request bounded: name the exact artifact, person, or release decision affected and the smallest action needed. Agents may summarize evidence and recommend a response; a human owner makes access, moderation, licensing, governance, and release decisions. Do not include private keys, credentials, or confidential material.
