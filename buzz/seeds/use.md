# Improve one task you already know

Run one bounded comparison:

- **Before:** how the task works today.
- **Change:** the AI-assisted step you will try.
- **Check:** how you will inspect the output.
- **Human judgment:** what a person still decides.
- **Result:** measured, observed, proposed, or unknown.

**Example (hypothetical):** Draft one meeting brief from approved notes, then
compare every decision, owner, date, and unsupported addition with the source.

Share enough for another Practitioner to reproduce the method without exposing
private or client information. Invite one specific improvement or check.
