# Start Here

**Practice is the open community for AI practitioners.**

We are here to learn AI, build with it, use it in real work, and share what actually works.

## Begin

1. Read the Practice principle: **Learn it. Build it. Use it. Share it.**
2. Introduce yourself with your role, current capability, and one real problem.
3. Choose the channel matching your next outcome: Learn, Use, Automate, Build, or Transform.
4. Apply one Practice or bring one workflow to the community.
5. Return with evidence, including failures.

## Community standard

Do not optimize for sounding informed. Optimize for becoming capable and helping the next Practitioner.
