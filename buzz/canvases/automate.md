# Automate

Turn repeated work into a reliable, observable, human-reviewable workflow. Start with a clear step before attempting autonomy.

## Belongs here

- Workflow decomposition, trigger, inputs, tools, outputs, and owner.
- Approval boundaries, evaluation checks, failure paths, retries, and rollback.
- A small test or prototype that another Practitioner can inspect.

## Keep out

- “Automate everything” proposals without a bounded workflow.
- Designs that conceal human responsibility or lack a recovery path.
- Secrets, production data, or scheduled Buzz workflows as a launch dependency.

## First action

Map one repeated workflow in five lines: trigger, steps, decision requiring approval, failure path, and observable success check.
