# Announcements

One canonical, low-volume stream for updates that change what the community should know or do.

## Belongs here

- Releases of Practice artifacts or projects.
- New Guides, Practices, Sessions, and major community decisions.
- Changes to contribution, access, or operating rules.

## Keep out

- Open-ended discussion, promotion, and model-news summaries.
- Drafts that have not been approved for publication.
- Duplicate updates better placed in a working channel.

## First action

Before posting, confirm the update is canonical, name the change and effective date, and give readers one next action or source to inspect.
