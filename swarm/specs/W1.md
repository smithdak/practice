## Requirements

- Reject a packet declaring more autonomy than the registry grants its agent.
- Fail when a repository check runs nowhere and carries no recorded exemption.

- Change no owner gate, operating hold, `maturity`, or `evidence_quality` value.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every gate and hold still reads OPEN and no agent is enabled.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
