# O3 — Hosted-surface inspection checklist

- **Wave:** 8
- **Lane:** release
- **Recommended model tier:** cheap
- **Mode:** build
- **Dependencies:** none

## Objective

Give the owner a fillable record for manually inspecting the hosted Buzz surface after the owner-operated apply.

## Read

- `AGENTS.md`
- `docs/CONTEXT.md`
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`
- `docs/NON_GOALS.md`
- `docs/QUALITY_BAR.md`

## Owned outputs

- `release/HOSTED_INSPECTION.md`
- `swarm/handoffs/O3.md`

## Scope rule

This is a build task. Change only the owned outputs and the handoff.

## Requirements

- Cover channels, canvases, seeds, membership visibility, and the start-here path.
- Record only non-secret evidence.
- State that an agent may not perform the apply or the inspection.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every member-visible surface has an inspection line.
- [ ] The record asks for no secret or participant data.
- [ ] The owner-only boundary is stated in the document.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task O3 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `swarm/handoffs/O3.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
