# G4 — Contributor intake, consent, and redaction templates

- **Wave:** 7
- **Lane:** community
- **Recommended model tier:** balanced
- **Mode:** build
- **Dependencies:** none

## Objective

Give Story and Workflow Clinic contributors a consent record and a redaction checklist before anything is published.

## Read

- `AGENTS.md`
- `docs/CONTEXT.md`
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`
- `docs/NON_GOALS.md`
- `docs/QUALITY_BAR.md`

## Owned outputs

- `templates/INTAKE_CONSENT.md`
- `templates/REDACTION_CHECKLIST.md`
- `swarm/handoffs/G4.md`

## Scope rule

This is a build task. Change only the owned outputs and the handoff.

## Requirements

- Record informed consent for publication, including what the contributor is consenting to.
- Cover confidential material, personal data, client identity, and unlicensed content.
- Keep both templates usable without any tooling.

## Acceptance

- [ ] The consent record states exactly what is being consented to and by whom.
- [ ] The redaction checklist covers every category of restricted material.
- [ ] Neither template asks for material that must not be stored in Git.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task G4 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `swarm/handoffs/G4.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
