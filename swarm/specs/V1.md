## Scope rule

This is a build task. Change only the owned outputs and the handoff.

## Requirements

- Follow the `skills/evals` conventions rather than inventing a second dialect.
- Cover injection, key requests, publication, maturity change, removal, participation-as-evidence, and vendor endorsement per agent.
- Give every case an id, input, expected, forbidden, and severity.
- Record no results and state that a defined case is not evidence of a pass.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every registered agent has a suite and every suite names a registered agent.
- [ ] Dropping a required adversarial topic fails validation.
- [ ] No case contains a credential, an address, a handle, or a real person's name.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task V1 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `swarm/handoffs/V1.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
