- **Wave:** 13
- **Lane:** operations

## Objective

Give an unattended run a record that says what it read, every path it wrote, and how to reverse it.

## Scope rule

This is a build task. Change only the owned outputs and the handoff.

## Requirements

- Record enough for the demotion checker to evaluate the ladder's observable triggers.
- Reject an entry that omits its reversal or records no preconditions.
- Keep the ledger append-only: a correction supersedes an earlier entry, never edits it.
- State that a valid entry is a record, not an authorization.

## Acceptance

- [ ] An entry missing its reversal fails validation.
- [ ] The committed sample is hypothetical and depicts no promoted run.
- [ ] The append interface round-trips and refuses to overwrite.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task X1 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `swarm/handoffs/X1.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
