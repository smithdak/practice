## Scope rule

## Requirements

- Require a reversal that has been executed rather than asserted.
- State the evidence that does not exist as well as the evidence that does.
- Ground every dossier claim in the script that implements the operation.
- Recommend no promotion; say plainly where a candidate should stay where it is.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every candidate dossier states write scope, reversal, blast radius, and how it could go wrong.
- [ ] Neither document contains a filled-in signature or implies a decision was made.
- [ ] A candidate unsuitable for unattended running is identified as such.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task X6 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `swarm/handoffs/X6.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
