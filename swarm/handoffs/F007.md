## Decisions made

- Content credit identifies the artifact, source, CC BY 4.0 license, and changes, with copy-ready formats for reuse and adaptations.
- Code reuse follows Apache-2.0 notice, license-copy, and modified-file obligations; it does not require the CC BY content-credit format.
- Contributions use a submission certification rather than a CLA. Contributors certify authority, provenance disclosure, and release under the applicable default license unless they conspicuously state otherwise before acceptance.
- Third-party and generated material require human provenance and rights review before merge; generated output is not treated as proof of permission, authorship, accuracy, or compatibility.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The repository does not yet define a pull-request template or metadata location for recording third-party source and license details. Contributors can record them in the file, its metadata, or NOTICE as the policy permits.
- License compatibility can be fact-specific. The policy directs uncertain material not to be merged or redistributed rather than making compatibility determinations.
