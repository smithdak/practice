COMPLETE

## Summary

Created a launch-safe metrics contract that connects Practice's north star to
activation, contribution, artifact reuse, implementation, evidence, response
quality, retention, and maintainer health. It distinguishes leading from
lagging indicators, requires inspectable evidence, excludes sensitive and
vanity data, and provides a spreadsheet/Markdown-only manual review method.

## Decisions made

- Use evidence-linked operational definitions instead of benchmarks or targets;
  no targets were invented.
- Treat durable artifact reuse and evidenced implementation as lagging outcomes;
  treat observable first value, contribution, and response review as leading
  signals.
- Operate launch measurement manually with aggregate reporting and voluntary,
  sanitized evidence.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- Reuse and implementation are partly voluntary/self-reported at launch and
  may remain unknown; the document requires that uncertainty be labeled.
- Maintainers still need to choose a review period and access-controlled ledger
  location; those choices are operational and intentionally not hard-coded.

## Deferred opportunities

- Consider repository-native aggregation or analytics only after manual review
  establishes a stable measurement method and a clear privacy need.
- Develop a lightweight contributor feedback instrument if the community later
  needs comparable longitudinal evidence, subject to explicit consent and
  privacy review.
