## Decisions made

- Capability labels classify an artifact's demonstrated outcome, never a person's status, access, or seniority.
- Each artifact has one primary type and primary capability; companion artifacts and narrowly justified secondary tags preserve distinctions without losing relationships.
- Role tags use the audience roles established in `docs/CONTEXT.md`; prerequisite knowledge is described in content instead of encoded as status tags.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The current Practice, Guide, Lab, and Story templates do not separate primary from secondary tags. The taxonomy preserves their existing fields and treats the fuller record as a recommended convention until a template owner elects to change it.
