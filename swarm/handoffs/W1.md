## Summary

Joined the Phase 3 contracts and runners into one operating procedure, enforced
the cross-contract invariants no single task owned, wired the new checks into CI
and the Makefile, wired the nine Phase 3 tasks into the manifest, and recorded
the phase.

The substantive change is the cross-contract bound. Each contract's own

which declared `recommend` for the Research Auditor against a registry grant of
`draft` — the exact drift the join was written to catch.

## Decisions made

- The autonomy bound lives in the packet validator rather than a separate
  script, because the validator already loads the registry and a second loader
  would be a second thing to keep in step.
- An agent absent from the registry, or a registry entry with no autonomy value,
  skips the bound rather than failing it. Guessing a bound would be worse than
  not having one.
- The wiring guard uses a recorded exemption list rather than an allowlist of
  CI steps, so adding a script forces a decision about where it runs instead of
  silently passing.
- The cadence and metrics runners stay out of CI. T1's reasoning holds: a report
  that behaves like a build gate creates work no person owns.
- The corrected worked example moved to `draft`, the level the registry grants
  the Research Auditor, rather than widening the registry grant to match the
  example. The registry is derived from the profile; the example is
  illustrative.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- **The Steward's posting condition is not observable.** Its profile says to
  draft rather than post when a reply is ambiguous or consequential, with no
  stated boundary. It is the only agent with a write surface, so this is the one
  bound that exists on paper and in nothing checkable. Recorded as finding 2 in
  the phase report; it needs a human decision before the Steward is enabled.
- **Five profile terms carry weight in guardrails without being defined**, listed
  as finding 3.
- **The eval cases have never been run.** They constrain nothing until they are,
  and a run needs owner gate 6.
- **The ladder mapping's build row bounds an assignment, not an identity**, since
  no persistent builder profile exists.
