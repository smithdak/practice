## Decisions made

- Git is the canonical record for governance decisions; Buzz discussion must be summarized in Git when it informs a decision.
- At launch, the founder is the sole governance authority, while human maintainers may decide routine reversible work in their established areas.
- Demonstrated stewardship, rather than activity volume, is the basis for a founder-approved, scoped maintainer appointment.
- A successor cannot assume reserved authority without documented, lawful transfer of control.

## Risks or unresolved questions
