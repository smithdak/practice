## Summary

The autonomy ladder's A3 section requires a review point at which a bound is
renewed or withdrawn, says silence is not renewal, and demotes on an action
taken after the review point passed without a renewal record. Phase 4 finding 5
recorded that none of that was evaluable: no promotion field held a review
point, and no renewal record existed. This task closes that gap and promotes
nothing.

**Nothing is promoted, and no unattended run has occurred.**
`ops/autonomy/promotions.yaml` still reads `kill_switch: engaged` and
`promotions: []`; only its header comment changed. `ops/autonomy/renewals.yaml`
is new and ships `schema_version: 1`, `renewals: []`. Every catalogued
operation is still refused. No owner gate, operating hold, `maturity`,
`evidence_quality`, or agent status changed, and no ledger entry was written
outside temporary test directories.

What changed:

- **`review_point` is a required promotion field.** The guard refuses a
  promotion without one, or with one that is not an ISO date strictly after
  `signed_on` (`review-point-recorded`).
- **A renewal is a new record, never an edit.** `ops/autonomy/renewals.yaml`
  holds entries with exactly `operation`, `promotion_signed_on`, `renewed_on`,
  `review_point`, `signed_by`, `reviewed`. The guard validates every entry and
  refuses every operation on any defect (`renewal-record-readable`): missing or
  unreadable file, unknown or missing field, a renewal naming no promotion, a
  future `renewed_on`, `renewed_on` before the promotion's `signed_on`,
  `review_point` not after `renewed_on`, a signer without reserved authority, a
  `reviewed` path that does not exist, or two renewals of one promotion on the
  same day.
- **The guard refuses a passed review point** (`review-point-not-passed`). The
  effective review point is the promotion's own or the `review_point` of the
  latest renewal whose `renewed_on` is on or before the as-of date. The refusal
  says silence is not renewal. Refusals now also list the preconditions that
  held, so every verdict names all three new checks.
- **The runner records the effective review point** the guard computed as
  `promotion.review_point`, not the raw promotion field, and reads
  `renewals.yaml` into `paths_read`.
- **The ledger requires `review_point`** on a recorded promotion;
  `promotion: none` is unchanged. `ops/ledger/SAMPLE_run.md` records
  `promotion: none` and needed no change.
- **The demotion check evaluates A3-3.** `acted-after-review-point` is
  `evaluable=True`, `basis=evaluated-here`, with a detector: an acting entry
  whose `run_date` is after its recorded `promotion.review_point` fires, unless
  `renewals.yaml` carries a renewal of that promotion covering the run date, in
  which case the finding is a record disagreement (a stale review point was
  recorded). An acting A3 entry with no readable review point is an unjudged
  record. 16 triggers, 6 evaluable, 10 not.
- **Documents.** The autonomy README's record table, guard-check table,
  promotion steps, and reversal order; proposal section 9 and the transcription
  step; the review contract's review-point sentence, checked-by-machine table,
  and first check; the operating loop's record table; the ledger schema's
  promotion table, rule 6, and trigger table.

## Decisions made

1. **With no promotion, the two review-point checks fail rather than go
   unevaluated.** The spec requires every shipped verdict to list
   `review-point-recorded` and `review-point-not-passed` as checked or failed.
   A missing promotion has no review point, so both fail closed, and the
   shipped refusal set is now four preconditions instead of two.
   `test_shipped_catalog_passes_its_own_structural_checks` was updated
   deliberately, and the README's example output shows the new verdict. The
   runner's safety-invariant test still asserts `kill-switch-released` and
   `promotion-signed` are named.
2. **Refusals list the preconditions that held.** The refusal render gained a
   `preconditions held:` line. Without it a refused verdict could never show
   `renewal-record-readable`, and a reviewer reading a refusal should see what
   did hold. The scheduled workflow reads only the exit code, so it is
   unaffected.
3. **A renewal signer needs reserved authority.** The spec says "a controlled
   operating role, the same vocabulary the guard already applies to
   promotions". The guard applies two checks to a promotion signature (role
   vocabulary, then `founder`/`beta-owner`/`continuity-owner`); renewing an A3
   bound is the same reserved decision made again, so a renewal takes both.
   `maintainer` is refused with a message saying why.
4. **A renewal dated after the as-of date is a record defect to the guard.**
   The effective-review-point rule ignores such a renewal (implemented in
   `effective_review_point` and unit-tested), but the guard's as-of date is its
   "today", exactly as it is for `signed_on`, so `renewed_on` after it is
   refused as signed in the future. The demotion check is where "ignored for
   that date" does real work: a renewal recorded after a run does not cover
   it.
5. **A renewal identifies its promotion by `(operation, promotion_signed_on)`
   in both the guard and the demotion check.** A renewal naming a signing date
   no promotion carries refuses the record; a renewal whose
   `promotion_signed_on` differs from an entry's `promotion.signed_on` does not
   cover that run.
6. **Two renewals of one promotion dated the same day are refused.** The spec
   leaves it open; an ambiguous "latest" is the kind of edge the guard should
   refuse rather than pick.
7. **The demotion check reads `renewals.yaml` leniently and live.** It is the
   one record read as it stands rather than as the run observed it, because a
   renewal is by design added after the promotion and may be added after a run.
   An entry it cannot date covers nothing and is noted; a missing or unreadable
   file excuses nothing. Whether the file is well-formed is the guard's
   question. The schema states this exception.
8. **An acting entry with `promotion: none` is not additionally reported as
   unjudged for the review point.** `acted-outside-recorded-bound` already
   fires on it, so it is never counted as clear; the unjudged case is reserved
   for an acting A3 entry whose promotion mapping has no readable
   `review_point`.
9. **A promotion the runner cannot record in full is recorded as
   `promotion: none`.** The existing precedent for a malformed promotion was
   extended: the ledger requires `review_point`, so a promotion without one, or
   with a date that is not a date, is recorded as none and the guard's refusal
   is in `preconditions`. When the guard did compute an effective review point,
   the entry carries that value.
10. **`normalize_date` rejects a YAML timestamp.** The records carry calendar
    dates; a `2026-12-02T09:00:00` review point is refused rather than
    truncated. `normalize_signed_on` remains as an alias.
11. **Two adjacent corrections in owned files.** `ops/autonomy/README.md` and
    `ops/autonomy/PROMOTION_PROPOSAL.md` said "seven" permanently ineligible
    operations; the ladder has listed six since amendment 001. Corrected to
    six. The ops loop and runner docstring now say twenty-seven preconditions.
12. **As-of dates** on the five edited documents moved to 2026-09-03, because
    their content changed on that date.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- **The demotion check's disagreement finding cannot tell back-dating from a
  runner defect.** A covering renewal with an entry recording a stale review
  point means either the renewal was recorded after the run and dated earlier,
  or the runner recorded the promotion's own review point instead of the
  effective one. The finding says both and asks a human to compare Git
  history; nothing here can decide it.
- **`swarm/reports/PHASE4_REPORT.md` and `swarm/handoffs/X2.md` say
  "twenty-four preconditions" and "exactly those two" refusals.** Both are
  dated records and were not edited. This handoff is the record that the count
  is now twenty-seven and the shipped refusal set is four.
- **The guard's as-of date is its only notion of "today".** An audit run with
  `--as-of` in the past will refuse on any renewal signed after that date, as
  it already did for a promotion signed after it. That is consistent, but a
  reader expecting `--as-of` to answer "would this have been permitted on date
  X" should know the renewal record is judged against X too.
- **No CI or Makefile wiring gap found.** The guard, ledger validator, and
  demotion check already run in `make checks` and `.github/workflows/ci.yml`;
  the runner and guard are exempt in `ValidatorWiringTests.EXEMPT` for stated
  reasons. Nothing new needed wiring.
- **Concurrent edits under `buzz/` by Z2** were in the working tree during
  every validation run. The one transient failure is recorded above; the final
  full suite passed with those edits present.

## Deferred opportunities

- **A `--as-of` for the demotion check.** Today it judges every recorded run
  against the renewal record as it stands. A date argument would let a
  reviewer ask what the check would have said on a past date.
- **A rate limit on renewals.** Nothing stops a renewal every day. The ladder's
  "rate and scope limit" is still only the scope half.
- **Recording the renewal that covered a run.** The entry records the
  effective review point but not which renewal supplied it. A
  `promotion.renewed_on` field would make the disagreement finding sharper; it
  is a ledger schema version change, not an edit.
- **A demotion-history record**, carried forward from X4: a fired trigger is
  reported, but nothing records that a human then withdrew or renewed.
- **Upgrading the inline `scripts/*.py` references in the autonomy documents
  to links**, now that the phase has landed; they were written as inline code
  when the scripts did not yet exist.
