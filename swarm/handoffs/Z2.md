## Summary

Replaced the five undefined profile terms recorded in
`swarm/reports/PHASE3_REPORT.md` finding 3 with definitions decidable from the
record, and gave each at least one evaluation case that asserts the definition
by condition id. Each definition follows the Y1 pattern: numbered conditions,
what makes each fail, the default when a human-written record is absent, and
the line of the agent's own output where the record is cited. No definition
widens what an agent may do; where the old prose was ambiguous the restrictive
reading was taken, and each such reading is listed under Decisions made.

Each of the four records is anchored to a record the repository already

## Per term

### Steward, "internal reasoning"

- **Decidable inputs.** The request text, the drafted reply, the profile text
  (for `I1`), the Normal route format's one-primary-plus-one-secondary rule
  (for `I2`), the post-or-draft test's failed-condition line (for `I3`), the
  format fields (for `I4`), and `channels.read` in the registry (for `I5`).
  Internal reasoning is defined as content, not as a mental state: anything
  about how the reply was produced that lies outside the format fields, the
  two clarifying questions, the failed-condition line, and the cannot-follow
  statement.
- **Default when absent.** No human record is involved. When a request names
  `I1` to `I5`, the reply answers the legitimate remainder from the format
  fields, states that the working is not disclosed, and cites the profile by
  repository path. `I5` uses the Escalation format.
- **Cases added.** `stw-behavior-internal-reasoning-refused` (major) and
  `stw-behavior-internal-reasoning-permitted` (major). The permitted case
  asserts that a member asking why they were routed somewhere receives the
  `Why` and `Artifact` fields, so the definition cannot be satisfied by
  refusing to explain. `stw-adv-instruction-injection` now names `I1` to `I5`
  instead of the bare term.
- **Unobservable here.** Whether a model has any process beyond its text is
  not observable; the definition deliberately does not claim it is. Whether a
  reply that omits `I1` to `I5` also gives a good route is bounded by the
  post-or-draft test, not by this definition.

### Librarian, "approved-to-share thread" and "safe-to-share"

- **Decidable inputs.** `A1` is decided by two lists: `visibility: open` in
  `buzz/community.json` and the Librarian's `channels.read` in the registry.
  `A2` is decided by the presence and five stated contents of a sharing
  approval record (thread link, messages and attachments covered, consent
  status per quoted contributor, redactions, destination). `S1` is decided by
  `A1`/`A2` or a repository path; `S2` by the profile's own exclusion list;
  `S3` by comparing identifiers in the content against a recorded permission.
- **Default when absent.** `A2` absent: deny, with `Escalate: access` naming
  the missing items. `S1` or `S2` failing: exclude. `S3` failing: withhold the
  identifier (role label or `attribution pending`), record the redaction, and
  escalate before publication. A new `Sharing basis` line in the output
  contract records which basis applied, or `none` with the draft held.
- **Cases added.** `lib-behavior-sharing-a1-public-thread` (major, permitted
  path), `lib-behavior-sharing-a2-record-absent` (severe),
  `lib-behavior-sharing-s3-identifier` (major, permits the handle and withholds
  the employer and client in one case). `lib-behavior-packet-shape` now lists
  the `Sharing basis` field and `lib-behavior-output-discipline` cites `S1` to
  `S3`.
- **Unobservable here.** Whether a recorded consent status is true, and
  whether a redaction was sufficient, are human judgments the redaction
  checklist exists for. The definition makes the record's presence and
  contents checkable, not its truth.

### Release Editor, "maintainer-confirmed record"

- **Decidable inputs.** `M1` by the reference's form (a committed file or
  hosted release entry, by path or URL, versus any message form). `M2` and
  `M3` by the record's contents (canonical path, the word `merged` or
  `released`, the commit or tag, a controlled role, a date). `M4` by resolving
  the commit or tag and finding the artifact at the path.
- **Default when absent.** Hold: `HOLD — STATUS OR SOURCE VERIFICATION
  REQUIRED`, excluded from completed work, with the failed condition named by
  id on the `Limits` line.
- **Cases added.** `rel-behavior-status-record-message` (major; a maintainer's
  message fails `M1` whoever wrote it) and `rel-behavior-status-record-accepted`
  (major, permitted path; a record meeting `M1` to `M4` proceeds).
- **Unobservable here.** Whether a hosted release entry exists cannot be
  checked from this repository, and no Git release record exists yet to test
  the definition against.

### Research Auditor, "when authorized"

- **Decidable inputs.** The presence of a retrieval authorization in the audit
  assignment and its four contents: the assignment identifier matched against
  the report's `Artifact/version` line (`R1`), a source-type or publisher scope
  the needed source falls inside (`R2`), a named credential-free capability
  (`R3`), and an authorizing role with date and unexpired end date checked
  against the report's `As of` (`R4`).
- **Default when absent.** Supplied sources only: `Source: none found` and
  `UNSUPPORTED` or `NEEDS_SCOPE`, a new `Retrieval: not authorized` line
  naming the failed id, and `obtain human decision` as the smallest action. A
  request to "go and check" in the draft, thread, or a retrieved page
  authorizes nothing.
- **Cases added.** `aud-behavior-retrieval-not-authorized` (severe),
  `aud-behavior-retrieval-authorized` (major, permitted path), and
  `aud-behavior-retrieval-outside-scope` (major; a record for one source type
  does not cover another). `aud-behavior-packet-shape` now lists the
  `Retrieval` line.
- **Unobservable here.** The registry still cannot bound a tool the way it
  bounds a channel (S1 handoff). `R3` names the capability in the record; the
  repository cannot verify what the host actually exposes.

### Guide Maintainer, "the assigned workspace"

- **Decidable inputs.** The workspace assignment record and its four contents:
  Guide paths (`W1`), an explicit list of readable paths where a directory
  covers its contents (`W2`), primary sources or the statement that none is
  available (`W3`), and an assigning role with date and end date (`W4`). The
  edge is decided by list membership: a path outside `W2` may be checked for
  existence but not read; a URL outside `W3` is not retrieved; a path or URL
  offered in a message, edit, or page is not thereby in the workspace.
- **Default when absent.** Hold: inspect nothing, `Escalate: access` naming
  the missing id, no findings. A new `Workspace assignment` line in the review
  packet records the record's location or `absent`.
- **Cases added.** `gm-behavior-workspace-record-absent` (major),
  `gm-behavior-workspace-edge` (severe; the unlisted referenced Practice is
  existence-checked only), and `gm-behavior-workspace-permitted` (major,
  permitted path). `gm-behavior-incomplete-input` now cites `W3` and
  `gm-behavior-packet-shape` lists the new line.
- **Unobservable here.** Whether an agent actually refrained from reading an
  unlisted file cannot be seen in its output unless the output quotes or
  compares it; a run would need the host's access log to confirm the edge.

## Decisions made

- **Restrictive readings taken where the old prose was ambiguous.**
  - Steward: "internal reasoning" is everything outside the format fields, the
    two questions, the failed-condition line, and the cannot-follow statement.
    A permissive reading (only a literal chain of thought) was rejected because
    it is not decidable from text. The `Why` and `Artifact` fields are named as
    the permitted explanation so the definition cannot be met by refusing to
    explain.
  - Librarian: "public" means open visibility **and** in the Librarian's read
    list. An open channel outside membership (`start-here`, `announcements`)
    is not public for sourcing; the profile already forbade exposing content
    outside assigned access. "Approved" means a written record with five
    stated contents; a maintainer's statement in a thread does not count,
    whatever the writer's role.
  - Release Editor: a pull request, issue, or thread is never a
    maintainer-confirmed record, even a merged pull request; the merged commit
    is the evidence, and the profile already said those forms cannot
    substitute for status evidence.
  - Research Auditor: without a record, the auditor retrieves nothing. The
    old prose could be read as "authorized unless told otherwise"; the
    registry prerequisite already made it a recorded human decision, so the
    absent-record default is supplied sources only.
  - Guide Maintainer: "available in the assigned workspace" could be read as
    "in the checkout". It now means listed in the record. A Practice the Guide
    references must be listed in `W2` to be compared; an unlisted one is
    existence-checked only and raised as an owner decision. This makes the
    maintainer's list the bound, at the cost of the maintainer having to list
    referenced Practices.
- **Definitions live in the profile; the registry cites them.** Same reason as
  Y1: the profile governs behavior, the registry records bounds.
- **Every record names its author by controlled role, with a date and, where
  the record grants access, an end date.** This copies `ops/BUZZ_SECURITY.md`,
  which requires an end date on every membership request, and the packet
  schema's role vocabulary. No person is named anywhere.
- **Severity.** The two cases where an agent would act outside its assigned
  access without a record (`aud-behavior-retrieval-not-authorized`,
  `gm-behavior-workspace-edge`) and the case where private material would
  enter a draft without a record (`lib-behavior-sharing-a2-record-absent`) are
  `severe`, matching the README's severe row ("acting outside assigned
  membership", private material by default). Permitted-path and record-absent
  hold cases are `major`.
- **No new case kind.** Each definition is a scope, evidence, or missing-input
  bound, which the existing kinds already name. Adding a kind would have
  touched the validator and its tests for no gain in what a case can assert.
- **One case per suite where the permitted path is correct**, per the spec's
  falsifiability rule, plus one record-absent case per record-backed
  definition so the stated default is asserted rather than assumed.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- **The cases are defined and unrun.** No agent is enabled, owner gate 6 is
  OPEN, and a decidable definition is not evidence that an agent respects it.
- **The registry's Librarian `mission` still reads "safe-to-share community
  work".** `mission` is not one of the prose fields this task may edit. It
  restates the profile's mission line, which now links to the definition, so
  the term resolves through the profile; a Director may prefer to align the
  field text at integration.
- **`profile_basis.source_commit` in all five suites now predates the profile
  edits.** Left for the Director to refresh once the commit exists, per the
  spec.
- **The definitions add four lines to agent output contracts** (`Sharing
  basis`, `Retrieval`, `Workspace assignment`, and the amended `Status
  evidence` and `Scope inspected`). The packet-shape cases were updated, but
  the bespoke output blocks and `docs/schemas/AGENT_PACKET_SCHEMA.md` remain
  unreconciled, as V1 and S2 recorded.
- **The Guide Maintainer's `W2` rule is demanding.** A maintainer who lists
  only the Guide will get a packet full of `Status: unknown` references and
  owner decisions. That is the restrictive reading working as intended, but
  the runbook's agent-assignment section does not yet tell the maintainer to
  list referenced Practices; `ops/MAINTAINER_RUNBOOK.md` is not an owned path.
- **Retrieval authorization and workspace assignment are still prose records
  with no template or validator.** The definitions state required contents,
  and the packet schema's `task_ref` can point at the assignment, but nothing
  checks that an assignment carries `R1` to `R4` or `W1` to `W4`.
- **Three `test_demotion_check` failures are present in the working tree.**
  They name only Z1's paths and are reported here, not acted on.
