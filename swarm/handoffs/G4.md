## Summary

Created the two intake-time and publish-time gates that L002 and L004 both
deferred: `templates/INTAKE_CONSENT.md` records what a Practitioner shares,
what they must never share, six independent consent statements (publication
scope, CC BY 4.0 grant per docs/DECISIONS.md, attribution, withdrawal before vs.
after publication, human review, accuracy), a redaction-request table, and a
handle-only ledger row format. `templates/REDACTION_CHECKLIST.md` defines the
seven checks a contributor and a human reviewer each run on the exact
candidate version before a Story or Workflow Clinic artifact publishes, with a
two-column results table and separate sign-off lines that forbid
self-approval.

## Decisions made

- Completed consent records stay private (access-controlled maintainer
  location); only the redacted artifact and one non-secret ledger row become
  public. This follows the Phase 2 rule "no secrets or participant data in
  Git" and `ops/METRICS.md` retention red lines.
- Consent is six separate opt-in statements with default `no`, mirroring the
  consent model in `ops/FIRST_PRACTICE_SESSION.md`; withdrawal before
  publication is unconditional, while after publication the Git record
  persists and requests route to a human maintainer (Story `withdrawn` status
  per `docs/schemas/STORY_SCHEMA.md`).
- Added a consent-and-license check (#7) to the redaction checklist beyond the
  six checks named in the task, because the Story schema review checklist
  requires consent, organization status, and license verification before
  publication.
- The ledger row carries no personal data beyond the contributor's chosen
  handle; a `role-only` / `none` attribution choice keeps even the handle out
  of the published artifact if preferred.
- Both templates carry the required one-line governance routing for ambiguous
  legal situations, framed as operational commitments rather than legal
  claims.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- No named human maintainer or governance route exists yet (carried risk from
  L004); the templates define the process but cannot name who receives the
  private record.
- The intake ledger's concrete location (which spreadsheet or Markdown file)
  is undecided; Part 6 specifies format only.
- Checklist anchors assume GitHub-style heading slugs; other renderers may
  resolve them differently (the link checker validates file targets, not
  fragments).
- No real contributor has used these templates yet; first live use should be
  treated as a trial of the process, not a proven flow.

## Deferred opportunities

- A machine-readable intake schema plus validator (like `validate_artifacts.py`)
  that checks a submitted Story for front-matter license and organization
  fields before human review.
- A retention schedule and deletion runbook for completed private consent
  records.
- Extending intake/consent templates to Notes and Project contributions, which
  have lower sharing stakes but may still involve third-party material.
- A anonymized worked example of a completed consent record for onboarding
  documentation.
