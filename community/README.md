# Community

How people join Practice, contribute, get credit, and are governed. Every decision described in these files is made by a human. Practice is pre-launch: the community hub is not open and there is no public invitation route yet ([../README.md](../README.md), "Where this project is right now").

## Rules that hold across these files

- Git is the durable record; Buzz is for conversation. A Buzz thread does not approve, publish, or credit anything ([CONTRIBUTION_MODEL.md](CONTRIBUTION_MODEL.md)).
- Governance, licensing, access, moderation, and enforcement decisions are human-owned. A community agent may flag and route; it may not decide, merge, appoint roles, or remove people or content ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), [MODERATION.md](MODERATION.md)).
- A capability label names the next outcome for one piece of work, not a rank, credential, or permanent level ([CAPABILITY_SELF_ASSESSMENT.md](CAPABILITY_SELF_ASSESSMENT.md)).
- Recognition follows demonstrated usefulness; counts of posts, reactions, or activity confer no status or authority ([CONTRIBUTION_MODEL.md](CONTRIBUTION_MODEL.md)).
- Hosted Buzz communities are invite-only, and an invitation grants access, not a role or permission to act for Practice ([ONBOARDING.md](ONBOARDING.md)).
- Contributors are credited in Git: commits, pull request descriptions, co-authorship where applicable, and artifact acknowledgments for substantial work ([../CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md), "Attribution and recognition").
