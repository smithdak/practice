## Requirements

- Define required inputs, artifact links, status checks, output formats, and prohibited claims.

- Preserve evidence quality and uncertainty.

## Acceptance

- [ ] At least five evaluation scenarios.
- [ ] No unmerged work can be announced as complete.
- [ ] Every summary links to canonical artifacts.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task B011 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `handoffs/B011.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
