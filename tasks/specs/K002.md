## Requirements

- Explain when a collection becomes a Guide.
- Define update and deprecation rules.

- [ ] Template and schema agree.

- [ ] A Guide has a clear completion outcome.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task K002 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `handoffs/K002.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
