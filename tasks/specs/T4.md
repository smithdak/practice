## Scope rule

This is a build task. Change only the owned outputs and the handoff.

## Requirements

- Implement exactly the documented states and transitions; invent no new state.
- Refuse a record whose last actor is an agent sitting in a human-owned state.
- Route safety, privacy, access, conduct, and legal categories to a human in every state.
- Apply a field allowlist and scan for identity and credential leakage.

## Acceptance

- [ ] An agent cannot move a record into a human-owned state.
- [ ] A state missing its required evidence fails with the file, field, and fix named.
- [ ] The committed sample record is hypothetical and validates.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate.py --task T4 --root .` passes.
- [ ] `handoffs/T4.md` records status, validation, decisions, risks, and deferred opportunities.
- [ ] The worktree is committed and clean.

## Stop conditions

Stop and write a `BLOCKED` handoff rather than guessing when a locked decision conflicts with this task, a current claim cannot be verified, required evidence is unavailable, or completing the task requires editing an unowned path.
