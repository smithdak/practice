# Canonical Context

## Product

**Practice** is an open-source community for people putting AI to useful work.

It begins as a community and public body of knowledge, not a consultancy, course business, or software platform. Buzz is the operating hub where humans and agents work together. Public, durable artifacts remain in Git so they can be discovered, versioned, forked, reviewed, and reused outside the relay.

## Positioning

> **Practice**  
> The open community for AI practitioners.

## Ethos

> **Learn it. Build it. Use it. Share it.**

## Mission

Make practical AI capability open and accessible to everyone.

## North star

Build the open standard for becoming AI-native.

## Audience

Practice is for the people responsible for making AI actually work:

- individual practitioners and beginners who want concrete capability;
- engineers, architects, builders, and operators;
- founders and professional-services leaders;
- internal AI champions and transformation leads;
- consultants and agencies implementing AI for clients;
- executives redesigning teams and organizations.

Practice is not optimized for passive AI spectators, model-release chatter, or prompt collecting.

## Capability ladder

1. **Learn** — understand models, context, tools, limitations, security, and evaluation.
2. **Use** — integrate AI into real daily work.
3. **Automate** — turn recurring work into reliable, reviewable workflows.
4. **Build** — engineer agents, tools, and AI-native systems.
5. **Transform** — redesign teams and organizations around new capabilities.

## Fundamental artifact types

- **Practice** — a reusable, tested method.
- **Guide** — an opinionated path combining multiple Practices.
- **Lab** — a reproducible experiment or evaluation.
- **Story** — a real implementation with before, intervention, result, and lessons.
- **Note** — a smaller observation that may mature into a Practice.
- **Project** — open-source software or infrastructure built by the community.

## First flagship guide

**The AI-Native Practitioner** should take someone from casual AI use through reliable automation, agentic engineering, and organizational transformation.

## First three Practices

1. Build a reusable context pack for recurring work.
2. Redesign a recurring workflow into a human-reviewed AI workflow.
3. Verify an agent's output before accepting or shipping it.

## Community behavior

The highest-status action is:

> I learned something useful and made it easier for the next person.

Discussion is valuable when it produces clearer decisions, reusable knowledge, working artifacts, or measured outcomes.
