# Non-Goals for the Initial Build

The swarm must not expand the first release into any of the following:

- a custom community platform;
- a replacement for Buzz;
- a course marketplace or LMS;
- a certification or credentialing program;
- a paid membership system;
- a consulting-services website;
- an AI news aggregation product;
- a giant prompt library;
- a model leaderboard without a concrete Practice use case;
- a production SaaS application;
- a mobile app;
- a complex brand identity or logo project;
- a broad directory of every AI tool;
- autonomous moderation, banning, or content deletion;
- self-hosting Buzz before hosted operation proves insufficient;
- scheduled Buzz workflows as a launch dependency.

A worker encountering an attractive adjacent idea should record it in its handoff under **Deferred opportunities**, not implement it.
