## Decisions made

- Kept all discovery, reuse, and contribution paths public in Git; an
  invitation only grants Buzz coordination access.
- Required a human-tested private request route before soliciting broad access
  requests, instead of adding a custom application or collecting requests in
  public threads.
- Made public-request review a lightweight, participation-focused safety and
  capacity check, not a credential, status, or contribution-volume filter.
- Assigned invitation issuance, revocation, membership, and enforcement to
  humans; agents may only route or summarize sanitized information.
- Treated invitation issuance and joining as operational events, not health
  metrics; post-entry Activation and Response quality use `ops/METRICS.md`.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The verified Buzz snapshot establishes invite-only hosted communities but
  does not document stable invitation expiry, revocation, or bulk-invite
  controls. The responsible human must check current hosted documentation or
  support before relying on any such control.
- No private, human-monitored public request route is named in the repository.
  Broad invitation promotion must remain paused until the owner establishes
  and tests one; public Git access remains available in the meantime.
