## Decisions made

- Private reports use the community's published private route; a named human maintainer must own that route before public participation opens.
- Quality and scope issues receive visible normal-review actions and are not treated as conduct violations without independent conduct evidence.
- Community agents can flag and route concerns but cannot investigate, decide, warn, restrict, remove, ban, or resolve appeals.
- Every content-removal or access action is human-owned and explained visibly when privacy and safety permit.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The current repository does not identify a specific named private reporting contact. Launch operations must publish one before opening public participation.
- Retention duration and secure storage location for private moderation records are not defined by this task and require a human-owned operational decision.
