## Summary

Expanded the Release Editor profile into a deployable, human-reviewed drafting
contract for announcements and weekly release briefs. It requires canonical
artifact links and merged/released status evidence, preserves evidence levels
and uncertainty, and separates held or proposed work from completed releases.

## Decisions made

- Use a per-item completion gate with canonical artifact, status evidence,
  source state, evidence state, and limits.
- Require human review before publication and keep posting, merging, and
  moderation outside the agent's authority.
- Provide separate announcement and weekly release brief output formats.
- Treat missing status or links as a hold, and use `Not measured` when no
  outcome evidence is supplied.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- A maintainer must supply and verify the status record and canonical link for
  each release item; the profile cannot resolve missing provenance by guessing.
- Publication destination and final wording remain human decisions.

## Deferred opportunities

- Add a repository-backed release index or automated link/status checker after
  a maintainer defines the source format and authority for release records.
