## Decisions made

- Replaced the draft `capabilities` list with one required primary `capability` and optional `secondary_capabilities`, matching the taxonomy rule that an artifact has one primary capability.
- Used `draft`, `published`, and `deprecated` as Guide lifecycle states; published Guides require `last_verified`.
- Defined a Guide as a sequenced, evaluated path integrating multiple related Practices toward one clear completion outcome.
- Required module-level Practice references and completion checks, with canonical Practices remaining the source for method details, failure modes, and evidence.
- Used semantic versioning with patch/minor/major rules tied to whether the path or completion outcome changes.
- Preserved deprecated Guides for history and made replacement links optional so unsafe or inaccurate paths can be retired without inventing a successor.
