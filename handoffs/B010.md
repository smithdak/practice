## Summary

Expanded the Research Auditor profile into a deployable, narrow evidence and
citation-integrity agent. It now defines a primary-source hierarchy, claim IDs,
scope and as-of date checks, contradiction handling, explicit dispositions, a
compact report format, and seven evaluation cases. It explicitly prohibits
invented citations, unsupported extrapolation, strategy rewriting, and
publishing or moderation actions.

## Decisions made

- Used a stable claim-ID and disposition vocabulary so reports identify exact
  claims and their action status.
- Defined source quality by authority and scope match, while allowing vendor
  documentation only for claims about that vendor's stated behavior.
- Kept strategy, corrections, human review, and publication outside the
  auditor's authority.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- A source may be inaccessible, undated, or contradictory; the profile makes
  those conditions explicit and requires uncertainty or human escalation rather
  than inference.
- Legal or other consequential claims still require qualified human review;
  this profile is an evidence check, not professional advice.

## Deferred opportunities
