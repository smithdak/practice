## Decisions made

- Retained the baseline channel count; no channel met a demonstrated need for
  addition, merge, or removal at launch.
- Kept `foundry` and `maintainers` private for construction and maintainer
  work, respectively.
- Kept every launch channel as a stream, consistent with the Buzz platform
  snapshot and launch constraints.
- Defined routing through the Learn → Use → Automate → Build → Transform
  capability ladder, with separate paths for questions, projects, and evidence.
- Added evidence thresholds and maintainer review requirements for creating,
  renaming, archiving, or splitting channels.
