## Decisions made

- Began with deterministic automation and human review, treating agent use as a narrowly justified escalation rather than a default architecture.
- Defined autonomy as a per-step design variable, with explicit no-agent decision rules.
- Kept the module model-, provider-, framework-, and scheduler-agnostic; examples are explicitly hypothetical and do not assert measured results.
- Required a rollback, compensating action, or containment path for every permitted external effect, with inspection or testing evidence distinguished from proof of general reliability.
