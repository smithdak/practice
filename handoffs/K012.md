## Summary

Created Practice 003, a reusable acceptance gate for agent-produced research, content, code, configuration, and operational work. It ties acceptance criteria to evidence, requires universal and artifact-specific checks, preserves a rollback path, blocks unsupported completion claims, and defines mandatory human approval before consequential effects.

## Decisions made

- Set maturity to `proposed` and evidence quality to `none`; the repository contains a method and hypothetical example, not a completed trial.
- Used `pass`, `fail`, and `unknown` as explicit check states; mandatory failures or unresolved unknowns block `accept`.
- Required human approval before publication, merge, deployment, external communication, data or permission changes, spending, or consequential decisions.
- Covered research/data, content, code/automation, configuration/prompt/policy, and operational actions while retaining universal checks for every artifact.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The Practice has not yet been trialed on real artifacts, so usability and effectiveness are unmeasured.
- Organizations must map “consequential,” domain authority, retention, and recovery-window requirements to their own policies.
- Automated checks can provide evidence only for their defined behavior and cannot replace domain judgment or approval.

## Deferred opportunities

- Add a repository-native schema validator or CLI that checks gate records without implying acceptance.
- Run and document cross-domain trials, including a real rollback rehearsal and independent reproduction.
- Provide organization-specific adapters for change management, regulated review, and access-controlled evidence stores.
