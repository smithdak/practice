## Decisions made

- Made a single `start-here` introduction the universal first action; the self-assessment and route cards make its next action concrete without making capability a member rank.
- Assigned invitations, identity setup, access changes, and all key or recovery-material handling to the member and accountable humans, never to agents.
- Defined follow-up as a manual thread-based loop so onboarding does not rely on scheduled workflows, forum automation, or a promised response time.
- Treated agent assistance as optional routing support that cannot make access, moderation, governance, or contribution-acceptance decisions.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The exact hosted invitation and identity-setup screens are intentionally not described because the verified platform snapshot does not establish a stable UI or support flow. The onboarding flow directs access failures to the issuing human instead.
- The launch availability and response capacity of a steward agent are not yet established. The self-directed path remains complete without one.
