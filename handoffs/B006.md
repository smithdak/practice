## Decisions made

- Treat direct messages, private channels, canvases, and uploads as hosted data
  subject to the same no-secrets rule.
- Keep private operating channels human-controlled by default; construction
  access is task-scoped and expires when the task ends.
- Define least-privilege memberships for all five launch community agent roles;
  Research Auditor and Release Editor receive no standing Buzz access beyond a
  specific approved assignment.
- Use documented hosted support for unverified identity-recovery or access
  controls instead of assuming administrative behavior.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The platform snapshot notes that hosted-community storage limits were not
  final in the support documentation reviewed on 2026-08-31. The runbook
  therefore makes no capacity assumption.
- The exact hosted identity-recovery and access-revocation interface should be
  confirmed with official support before it is needed.
