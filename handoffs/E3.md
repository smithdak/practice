## Risks or unresolved questions

- Late approval is structural in the swarm flow: worker commits land before
  the gate task runs, so this gate documents and rehearses recovery but
  cannot prevent the effect. Fixing this needs a swarm-flow decision (gate
  before merge), not a Lab.
- The checker's false-positive class (`weird(1).md` valid files reported
  broken) is latent: zero such filenames exist today, but one landing in a
  future PR would be blocked by CI with a confusing truncated message.
- Practice 003's own promotion bar is still unmet (second artifact type,
  refusal-path trial); no artifact may claim the gate is `tested`.
- `validate.py --task E3` cannot run until task wiring lands.
- No independent reproducer has re-executed the trial; reproduction steps are
  written for one but unverified by a second party.
