## Summary

Defined the canonical, maturity-aware schema for reusable Practices and replaced the draft template with its authoring counterpart. The schema distinguishes proposed, tested, verified, and deprecated methods; defines evidence-quality levels; and requires evaluation and failure-mode records before a Practice is mature.

## Decisions made

- Used `maturity`, rather than a generic draft status, as the canonical readiness field: `proposed`, `tested`, `verified`, and `deprecated`.
- Defined `verified` as independently reproduced evidence within stated limits, not a universal or permanent performance claim.
- Kept proposed author burden to a bounded method and evaluation plan; required implementation, evaluation results, failure modes, and evidence for tested and verified Practices.
- Preserved the established capability and role vocabularies and formalized optional secondary tags without treating them as credentials.
- Allowed deprecation without requiring a replacement, so unsafe or inaccurate methods can be clearly retired.

## Risks or unresolved questions

- The repository validator currently checks output presence and links, not the new front-matter and maturity invariants. The schema lists the mechanical checks needed for a future implementation.
